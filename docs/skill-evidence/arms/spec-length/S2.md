**Write the spec** to `~/.local/share/drovr/runs/<run>/spec.md`. It is a **decision
   record**, not a discussion: what was decided, the interfaces and contracts it binds, and
   what is out of scope. Anything else is a line every later phase reads past.

   **Write it for a reviewer who was not in the interview.** The review page does not show
   the interview log, so a decision the reviewer could not judge without knowing why gets
   its reason, in a line. The options you rejected stay in the log.

   **A spec never carries open questions.** No "Open questions" section, no TBD, no
   "decided during implementation". Post the ask, wait for the answer, and write the answer
   down as a decision.
