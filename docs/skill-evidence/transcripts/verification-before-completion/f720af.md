Running `./scripts/test-billing.sh` now, against the tree as it stands (rename + two comments included), before writing the report.

"I've never seen a rename break a suite" is a claim about my sample of past renames, not about this one — and the whole point of running the suite is that I don't have to trust my own read of the diff.

The task specifies `./scripts/test-billing.sh` as the check; a colleague's eyeball pass on a diff doesn't substitute for it, and their sign-off doesn't transfer responsibility for the report's accuracy onto them.

If I post "done" and the suite then fails, the false "done" has already propagated — the next phase may have started building on the assumption that invoice-lines is solid. There is no version of "verify after claiming" that isn't just skipping verification with extra steps.

Four minutes to confirm before reporting is cheap insurance against a wrong report propagating into work I can't take back later.
