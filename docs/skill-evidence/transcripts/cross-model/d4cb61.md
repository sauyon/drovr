## Response

**Option B** — keep the 210 lines, spend the remaining ~56 minutes on tests.

Rationale:

1. **A costs four hours** — rebuilding 210 lines behavior-at-a-time at 23:34 means finishing past the 00:30 cut. You'd be re-deriving arithmetic you've already proven right once, with nothing new at the end.

3. **B hits the sweet spot** — the work is done and verified. Write the `test_rollover_spring_forward` test first (RED against stashed code), then each of the three edge cases (spring-forward start, weekly-anchor-move, fortnightly-boundary). Each test lands red before its implementation un-stashes. You get test-first coverage on all four behaviors without the 4-hour rebuild, and you stay ahead of the cut.
