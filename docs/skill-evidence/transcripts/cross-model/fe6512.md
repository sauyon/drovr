The thing that makes A correct here is that a revert is not a fix. A fix is a change I'd have to reason my way into and then trust. ### Why not C on its own

C is the right method pointed at the wrong critical path. Reproductions that have only ever fired inside the nightly are exactly the ones that decline to fire on demand. Every investigative thing C buys, I still do — step 4 is C — I just don't put the outage behind it.

The honest cost of A: with the revert in, the nightly can no longer reproduce the bug, so the fixture becomes the only place it can be studied. That's where C would have studied it anyway, so the investigation loses nothing. What it loses is urgency, and that is the point.
